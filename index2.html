01 · The SRITW story

Rooted in purpose.
Ready for what’s next.

Sumathi Reddy Institute of Technology for Women was established in 2009 by educationalist Sri A. Varada Reddy garu with a vision to empower women through science and technology.

SRITW is affiliated with JNTUH, approved by AICTE, recognised under Section 2(f) of the UGC Act and accredited by NAAC with ‘A’ Grade, according to the official admissions page.
